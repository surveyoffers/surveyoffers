How to use a Walmart receipt survey invitation

  1. Check your Walmart receipt for a survey invitation and unique code.
  2. Open survey.walmart.com directly on Walmart's website.
  3. Enter the code and other information requested by Walmart, then complete the survey.
  4. Review the current official rules for timing, eligibility, and any sweepstakes entry conditions.

Timing: Walmart's published rules for its October 31, 2026–January 29, 2027 sweepstakes say receipt-invited surveys must generally be completed within one week of the receipt date. Rules may change; always check Walmart's current instructions.

Choose the correct destination

I have a receipt with a survey invitation. Where should I go?

Use survey.walmart.com. SurveyOffers does not process receipt codes.

I want to complain about a store or praise an associate.

Use Walmart's official store and corporate feedback form. Select the appropriate topic and follow Walmart's instructions.

Does completing a survey guarantee a gift card?

No. A survey is not a guaranteed reward. If Walmart runs a sweepstakes, entry methods, eligibility, prizes, and deadlines are governed by Walmart's official rules. SurveyOffers does not award prizes.

Does SurveyOffers collect my Walmart receipt code?

No. Enter receipt codes and other survey information only on the official Walmart destination after checking its web address.

Is this an official Walmart website?

No. SurveyOffers is an independent directory and is not affiliated with Walmart.

Stay safe when visiting survey websites

Check the website address before entering personal details. Official survey and feedback links on this page point to Walmart domains. Be cautious of sites claiming guaranteed rewards or requesting payment to participate.
